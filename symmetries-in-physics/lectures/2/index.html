Structure of groups, and a first look at crystallographic groups

Learning objectiveTurn this statement into questions you can answer.
  • We learn to compare groups by structure-preserving maps and to take groups apart: homomorphisms and their kernels, conjugacy and its geometric meaning, quotient groups and the first isomorphism theorem, direct products, and the construction of semidirect products from a normal subgroup and an action, and the decomposition of a finite group into simple constituents. As a first physical payoff we prove the crystallographic restriction theorem: a crystal lattice admits only two-, three-, four- and sixfold rotation axes.
