One map, two matrices

Consider reflection across the line y=xy=xy=x. Compare its matrix in the standard basis with its matrix in a basis aligned with the mirror.

  1. 01
    Act on the standard basis

    Find the images of (1,0)(1,0)(1,0) and (0,1)(0,1)(0,1) and use them as matrix columns.

  2. 02
    Align the basis

    Use (1,1)(1,1)(1,1) and (1,−1)(1,-1)(1,−1). What scalar does reflection apply to each?

  3. 03
    Explain the simplification

    Why is the second matrix diagonal even though the transformation has not changed?

Compare with the expected pattern

The standard matrix swaps coordinates. In the aligned basis, reflection fixes (1,1)(1,1)(1,1) and negates (1,−1)(1,-1)(1,−1), so its matrix is diag⁡(1,−1)\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)diag(1,−1).

The central thread

Fixing bases of VVV and WWW turns each T∈L(V,W)T\in\Lin(V,W)T∈L(V,W) into a matrix M(T)\Mat(T)M(T) whose columns are the images of the basis vectors in coordinates, and [Tv]C=M(T)[v]B[Tv]_{\mathcal C}=\Mat(T)[v]_{\mathcal B}[Tv]C​=M(T)[v]B​. Composition of maps becomes multiplication of matrices—which is what forces the row-by-column rule—so L(V,W)≅Fm×n\Lin(V,W)\iso\F^{m\times n}L(V,W)≅Fm×n and dim⁡L(V,W)=(dim⁡V)(dim⁡W)\dim\Lin(V,W)=(\dim V)(\dim W)dimL(V,W)=(dimV)(dimW). A map is an isomorphism iff it is bijective, and finite-dimensional spaces are isomorphic iff their dimensions agree, so every nnn-dimensional space is a copy of Fn\F^nFn. Changing basis replaces an operator's matrix AAA by the similar matrix P−1APP^{-1}APP−1AP; trace and determinant are similarity invariants and hence belong to the operator.

Learn the argument

Represent maps without confusing them with matrices

A linear map is geometric and basis-free; a matrix is its coordinate representation. Track the chosen bases at every step.

Misconception check

“A linear map has one intrinsic matrix.”

Which data, besides the map, are needed before its matrix entries are defined?

Reveal the test

You must choose an ordered basis for the domain and one for the codomain. Changing either changes the matrix, not the underlying map.
