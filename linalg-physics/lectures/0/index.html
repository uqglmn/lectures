Prepare

Know where you are going

Use these objectives as questions to answer by the end of the module.

  • Add, multiply, conjugate, and divide complex numbers, and move freely between Cartesian and polar form.
  • Multiply matrices, transpose them, and read off the trace, knowing which operations commute and which do not.
  • Evaluate 2×22\times22×2 and 3×33\times33×3 determinants and use det⁡A≠0\det A\neq0detA=0 as the test for invertibility.
  • Row-reduce a linear system, describe its solution set, and find a basis for the solutions of Ax=0Ax=0Ax=0.

The central thread

The four skills above are the computational floor the course is built on. From C\CC you need conjugate, modulus, division, and the polar form reiθre^{i\theta}reiθ, because everything quantum is complex. From matrix algebra you need multiplication (non-commutative!), transpose, and trace. Determinants give the one-number test det⁡A≠0\det A\neq0detA=0 for invertibility and, in Week 5, the characteristic polynomial det⁡(λI−A)\det(\lambda I-A)det(λI−A). Gaussian elimination solves systems and—reading a basis off the RREF of Ax=0Ax=0Ax=0—computes the null spaces, ranks, and dimensions that Lectures 1–4 turn into abstract structure. If the self-check exposes a gap, repair it here before Lecture 1.

  • 02Matrix algebra—(AB)ik=∑jAijBjk(AB)_{ik}=\sum_j A_{ij}B_{jk}(AB)ik​=∑j​Aij​Bjk​ (and AB≠BAAB\neq BAAB=BA); transpose ATA^{\T}AT; trace tr⁡(AB)=tr⁡(BA)\tr(AB)=\tr(BA)tr(AB)=tr(BA).
  • 03Determinants—2×22\times22×2 and 3×33\times33×3 by cofactors; det⁡(AB)=det⁡Adet⁡B\det(AB)=\det A\det Bdet(AB)=detAdetB; det⁡A≠0  ⟺  \det A\neq0\iffdetA=0⟺ invertible   ⟺  \iff⟺ columns independent.
  • 04Gaussian elimination—RREF, pivots vs.\ free variables; the null space of Ax=0Ax=0Ax=0 and a basis for it; rank as the pivot count.
