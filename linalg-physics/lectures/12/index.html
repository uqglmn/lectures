Tensors and the Tensor Product

4 learning objectives5 topics28 exercises

Before the lecture

What you will learn

  • Construct V⊗WV\tsr WV⊗W via its basis and universal property; spot non-factoring tensors.
  • Compute Kronecker products and use (A⊗B)(v⊗w)=(Av)⊗(Bw)(A\tsr B)(v\tsr w)=(Av)\tsr(Bw)(A⊗B)(v⊗w)=(Av)⊗(Bw).
  • Read a type-(p,q)(p,q)(p,q) tensor as a multilinear map and contract indices.
  • Distinguish product from entangled states in composite quantum systems.

After the lecture

Concept summary

The tensor product V⊗WV\tsr WV⊗W has basis { ei⊗fj }\set{e_i\tsr f_j}{ei​⊗fj​} and dimension (dim⁡V)(dim⁡W)(\dim V)(\dim W)(dimV)(dimW); its elements are sums of elementary tensors v⊗wv\tsr wv⊗w, most of which do not factor. In coordinates this is the Kronecker product, with (A⊗B)(v⊗w)=(Av)⊗(Bw)(A\tsr B)(v\tsr w)=(Av)\tsr(Bw)(A⊗B)(v⊗w)=(Av)⊗(Bw), tr⁡(A⊗B)=tr⁡A tr⁡B\tr(A\tsr B)=\tr A\,\tr Btr(A⊗B)=trAtrB, and eigenvalues λμ\lambda\muλμ. A type-(p,q)(p,q)(p,q) tensor is a multilinear map with ppp upper and qqq lower indices, unifying vectors, covectors, the metric, and operators (L(V,W)≅W⊗V∗\Lin(V,W)\iso W\tsr V^*L(V,W)≅W⊗V∗); contraction over a matched pair generalizes the trace. Within V⊗VV\tsr VV⊗V, the symmetric and antisymmetric tensors split the space and model bosons and fermions. In quantum mechanics composite systems live in HA⊗HB\Hil_A\tsr\Hil_BHA​⊗HB​, and entanglement is the existence of states that do not factor.
  • Tensor product V⊗WV\tsr WV⊗W—basis { ei⊗fj }\set{e_i\tsr f_j}{ei​⊗fj​}, dimension (dim⁡V)(dim⁡W)(\dim V)(\dim W)(dimV)(dimW); most elements do not factor.
  • Kronecker product—the coordinate form; tr⁡(A⊗B)=tr⁡A tr⁡B\tr(A\tsr B)=\tr A\,\tr Btr(A⊗B)=trAtrB, eigenvalues λμ\lambda\muλμ, size nm×nmnm\times nmnm×nm.
  • Type-(p,q)(p,q)(p,q) tensor—a multilinear map with ppp upper and qqq lower indices; unifies vectors, covectors, the metric, and operators (L(V,W)≅W⊗V∗\Lin(V,W)\iso W\tsr V^*L(V,W)≅W⊗V∗); contraction generalizes the trace.
  • Symmetric and antisymmetric tensors—split V⊗VV\tsr VV⊗V; model bosons and fermions.
  • Composite systems and entanglement—HA⊗HB\Hil_A\tsr\Hil_BHA​⊗HB​; entangled states are those that do not factor.
