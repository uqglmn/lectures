How it works

A companion, not a replacement for the notes

The platform brings together the official lecture PDFs, visual experiments, and ungraded self-checks. The current English LaTeX course remains the source of truth.

Learning data

No sign-in is required. Completed steps, question outcomes, and review dates are stored only in your browser. They are used to bring missed ideas back sooner and are not sent to the lecturer, university, or any third party.

The labels “needs review”, “developing”, and “retrieved independently” describe limited practice evidence; they are not grades or claims of permanent mastery.

Clearing site data or moving to another device will remove this local progress.

Content updates

When an English lecture or exercise LaTeX file changes, the build extracts its objectives, topic map, summary, and exercise statistics again. It also rebuilds the lecture PDF and a solution-free student exercise PDF.

Each week's visibility is controlled in one course.yml file. Hidden content is excluded from the public site bundle.

Core principles

  • English LaTeX is the content source
  • solutions are not published
  • accessible without an account
  • runs on static hosting
